Client feedback

“SUPPLIER SPEND ANALYSIS

We sent over 12 months of supplier data because we knew our total spending but couldn't easily see where the money was going. The analysis broke it down by category, supplier, and line item, then identified opportunities to consolidate purchases and consider alternative suppliers. Having that detail gave us specific places to start and a basis for comparing options. The changes we made following the analysis reduced indirect spend by roughly 7% to 30% across the categories we addressed.

Midwest Dental Office

“CONTRACT SUMMARY SHEETS

Our supplier agreements had different renewal dates and terms, which made it difficult to plan negotiations together. The contract summary brought the key provisions into one tracker, including assignment, change of control, and breach language. It helped us map out when to prepare for negotiations and where we could align renewal dates. That planning was particularly useful. We could assess alternatives across suppliers before committing to another term, instead of handling each renewal in isolation and finding ourselves locked in before we'd considered our options.

Multi-Location Automotive Business

“DASHBOARD + AUTOMATION PACKAGE

With several coffee shops, getting a clear picture of the business meant pulling together separate reports from each location. We now have one dashboard that brings the store data together and updates as new information comes in. We can compare sales across locations, follow trends, and see which stores need a closer look. Our operating reviews are more focused because we're looking at the same numbers in the same place. We spend less time assembling reports and more time discussing what's happening in the shops and what needs to change.

Mid-Atlantic Coffee Chain

Questions

What files do you need?

Spend analysis starts with accounts payable or purchasing data. Contract summaries need the agreements. Spreadsheet projects need a sample file and the intended output. For research or lead lists, send the questions or targeting criteria. Redact sensitive information from your initial sample.

When does the delivery period start?

On the next business day after scope and terms are accepted, all required inputs are complete, and payment is received.

What happens if you can’t deliver?

If we cannot provide the agreed work, the cancellation and correction-or-refund terms apply. An order without separately priced deliverables is refunded in full if we cannot provide conforming work.

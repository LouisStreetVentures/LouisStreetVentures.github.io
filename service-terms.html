General Service Terms

The terms that apply to every fixed-price service order.

Version 1, effective 2026-09-17

1. Payment

Payment is 100% upfront via Stripe in USD. Checks, wires, overpayments, and buyer-fee arrangements are not accepted. Work begins after payment and all required inputs are received.

2. Delivery start and client delays

The delivery clock starts on the next business day after payment and receipt of all required inputs. Business days are Monday through Friday, excluding US federal holidays. Missing inputs or unanswered scope questions pause the clock, and the client is notified.

3. Cancellation and refunds

You may cancel any time before the delivery clock starts for a full refund. After work begins, there are no refunds, but revision and error-correction rights still apply. If intake shows the order does not fit and no scope can be agreed, you receive a full refund and no work is performed. If required inputs are not provided within 30 calendar days of payment, a full refund is available on request. Refunds go to the original payment method within 10 business days.

4. Revisions versus additional scope

One consolidated revision request within five business days of delivery adjusts the agreed output. New data, metrics, questions, sources, workflows, or deliverables are additional scope and are quoted separately.

5. Error correction

Errors against the agreed scope are corrected free of charge when reported within 30 calendar days. Corrections do not consume the revision round.

6. Confidentiality and data handling

Client files are used only to fulfill the order and are never shared or sold. Raw client data is deleted from working storage within 90 days of delivery. Order confirmations and scope/terms versions are retained as business records.

7. Ownership

On payment, the client owns the final deliverables. Louis Street Ventures retains a copy for records and support.

8. Software and subscriptions

The client provides and pays for their own accounts and licenses. Subscription and usage fees are never included in service prices.

9. Post-delivery support boundaries

No meetings, presentations, ongoing monitoring, or recurring maintenance are included. Support is limited to the revision round and 30-day error correction.

10. Acceptance

Paying the Stripe checkout with the order reference from the written order confirmation confirms acceptance of that confirmation and these terms.

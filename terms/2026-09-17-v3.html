1. Who the agreement covers

These terms apply to business purchases of fixed-price services. “Provider,” “we,” and “us” mean the legal person or entity operating as Louis Street Ventures identified in your written order confirmation. “Client” and “you” mean the business identified there. The person accepting confirms that they are at least 18 and authorized to bind that business. These services are not offered for personal, family, or household use.

Before payment, we provide an order confirmation identifying both parties, the order reference, fee, agreed inputs, deliverables, exclusions, delivery schedule, and this terms version. The agreement consists of that confirmation, any attached service specification, and these terms. An order-specific change overrides these terms only if it expressly identifies the provision being changed and both parties agree in writing.

You accept by replying with explicit agreement to the order confirmation and the linked terms version, or by affirmatively accepting them at checkout. An inquiry or visit to this website does not place an order. Payment follows scope confirmation; work starts only after acceptance, payment, and complete inputs.

2. Scope and changes

We provide only the work and deliverables listed in the accepted order confirmation. Service-page limits apply as included in that confirmation. Examples, discussions, and sample deliverables do not expand the order or guarantee results.

The fee covers one complete input submission and the agreed platform, data structure, criteria, and output format. Additional or replacement data, new requirements, new integrations, extra deliverables, and changes to approved assumptions require a separate written agreement and fee before that work begins. We are not obliged to accept additional work.

3. Payment and scheduling

Fees are in USD and paid upfront through Stripe. Any applicable taxes or approved third-party costs are disclosed before payment. We do not accept overpayments or requests to pay another party on your behalf.

The delivery clock starts on the next business day after we confirm that acceptance, payment, and all required inputs are complete. Business days are Monday through Friday, excluding US federal holidays, using New York time. The error-reporting and correction periods are separate from the initial delivery period.

We notify you if missing inputs, unanswered questions, unavailable access, or approved scope changes affect the schedule. A client-caused pause extends the deadline by the pause and a restart interval disclosed in writing. If an essential dependency remains unavailable for 30 calendar days, we may close the order after seven calendar days’ written notice and apply the cancellation rules below.

You are responsible for the accuracy, completeness, legality, and authorized disclosure of your inputs and instructions. 5. Final delivery and error reports

Delivery occurs when the agreed files or access are made available and we send a delivery email identifying them, the error-reporting deadline, and any handoff steps. An inaccessible or materially incomplete delivery does not start the error-reporting period. Notify us promptly of access problems.

Delivery is final. No revision rounds, preference changes, or review meetings are included. You have five business days, starting on the next business day after delivery, to report an Error in writing with examples or reproduction steps. An “Error” is an objectively identifiable discrepancy, attributable to us, between the delivered work and the accepted specification, evaluated using the original inputs and agreed environment. Examples include a miscopied contract term, an incorrect calculation, or failure to implement an agreed cleaning rule.

A different preferred layout, wording, metric, category rule, research conclusion, or targeting choice is not an Error when the delivered work meets the accepted specification. New or corrected client inputs, changes to approved instructions, and changes to third-party systems after delivery are also additional scope. Addressing them requires a new written agreement and payment, and we may decline the work.

Inspect the delivered files during this period. Only the Error remedy in section 6 remains included after handoff; no general feedback or revision service is provided.

6. Error correction or refund

For a verified Error reported within the five-business-day period, we will correct or replace the affected work at no additional charge, or, at our choice, refund the fee for the affected deliverable and end that part of the order. Corrections are limited to making the original deliverable meet the accepted specification. A partial refund requires a separately priced deliverable in the accepted order; if the deliverable is not separately priced or the remaining work has no reasonable standalone use, the refund is the full order fee.

We acknowledge a timely Error report and state the resolution within five business days. Any chosen correction is delivered within ten business days of receiving the information and access reasonably needed to address it, unless both parties agree to a different date. If we cannot provide conforming work within that period, we provide the applicable refund rather than requiring an indefinite correction cycle.

After a corrected delivery, you have three business days, starting the next business day, to report that a previously reported Error remains unresolved or that the correction introduced an Error. This verification period permits no preference changes or additional work. If a verified Error remains, we provide the applicable refund; further correction work instead of a refund requires mutual written agreement.

Subject to section 15 and applicable law, this correction-or-refund process is the exclusive contractual remedy for failure of the deliverable to meet its agreed specification. It does not limit remedies for other duties, such as confidentiality, or rights that cannot lawfully be limited. You cannot recover twice for the same loss.

Handoff ends the scheduled project work. A conforming project is accepted when you expressly accept it in writing, when the five-business-day error-reporting period expires without a timely Error report, or when the three-business-day verification period expires without a timely report of an unresolved Error. An early acceptance does not shorten the original five-business-day Error remedy. A timely unresolved Error is handled under section 6 before that affected work is closed. We send a closing email recording acceptance or refund and the end of the engagement.

Except for timely Error reports handled under section 6, no work is included after handoff. No revisions, troubleshooting, technical support, maintenance, monitoring, data refreshes performed by us, retraining, migration, recovery, compatibility updates, or further meetings are included. There is no separate 30-day correction or support period. Any additional services require a new written agreement and payment, and we may decline them.

Completion ends the service obligation; it does not erase confidentiality, ownership, refund, recordkeeping, or other legal duties that survive. Error-reporting deadlines govern the included Error remedy and acceptance process. They do not excuse fraud or concealment, waive rights that cannot be waived, or turn non-delivery into completed performance.

If we cancel for reasons not caused by your breach, cannot perform the agreed project, or cannot resolve a material delivery failure under section 6, we refund the affected fee under the allocation rule in section 6. If we miss a committed initial delivery date for reasons within our control, you may give us five business days’ written notice to deliver; if we do not, you may cancel the undelivered work for the applicable refund. Client-caused delays are governed by section 3.

If no work has begun and required inputs have not been supplied within 30 calendar days after payment, you may request a full refund. These terms do not prevent a lawful payment dispute or require you to waive card-network or statutory rights. A payment dispute does not itself establish whether services conformed to the agreement.

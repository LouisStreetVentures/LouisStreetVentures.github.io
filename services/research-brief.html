Deep Research Brief

A written brief that answers one business decision or research question with cited public sources, clear evidence gaps, and a supported conclusion.

$590 / project
Delivery: five business days

Key limits

  • One written business decision or research question; up to five named alternatives.
  • Up to eight agreed comparison criteria; one geographic market.
  • English-language public web sources only; up to 15 substantive sources reviewed.
  • No interviews, surveys, vendor calls, or paid sources.
Start intake ↗

Eligible inputs and limits

  • One written business decision or research question.
  • Up to five named alternatives.
  • Up to eight agreed comparison criteria.
  • One geographic market.
  • English-language public web sources.
  • Up to three client background documents totaling 20 pages.

Included work

  • Review up to 15 substantive public sources.
  • Research the agreed criteria and document evidence gaps.
  • Distinguish verified facts, vendor claims, and analyst judgment.
  • Provide a supported conclusion when the evidence permits.
  • Report limitations instead of unsupported estimates.

Deliverables

  • Brief of up to six pages, excluding references.
  • Comparison table across the agreed criteria.
  • Source links with access dates.
  • Conclusion, key tradeoffs, and unresolved questions.

Excluded

  • Interviews, surveys, calling vendors, or requesting quotes.
  • Paid databases or paywall purchases.
  • Field research or ongoing monitoring.
  • Original market-sizing or financial models.
  • Investment recommendations.
  • Legal or medical research.
  • Exhaustive searches.

Revision allowance

Clarifications within the original question and criteria. A new alternative, criterion, geography, or question is additional scope.

Submit one consolidated request within five business days of delivery. After a revised delivery, a three-business-day verification period covers unresolved or newly introduced material failures only. No ongoing support, maintenance, or separate error-correction period is included after completion. See the review and completion terms.

Delivery clock rules

The delivery clock starts on the next business day after payment is received and all required inputs are in hand. Business days are Monday through Friday, excluding US federal holidays. If required inputs are missing or scope questions go unanswered, the clock pauses and you will be notified before work continues.

Client responsibilities

  • State the decision or question in writing and agree the criteria before research begins.
  • Provide background documents within the page limit.
  • Confirm the alternatives to be compared.

Contract Summary Sheets

Compare supplier terms and plan renewals before you commit to another agreement. Up to five agreements distilled into one summary workbook.

$690 / project
Delivery: five business days

Key limits

  • Up to five supplier agreements and 60 pages total, including amendments and exhibits.
  • English-language searchable PDFs or editable documents only.
  • Image-only scans, handwriting, and documents needing OCR cleanup are excluded.
  • Pricing extraction limited to 20 distinct price lines per agreement.
Start intake ↗

Eligible inputs and limits

  • Up to five supplier agreements.
  • Up to 60 pages total, including amendments and exhibits.
  • English-language searchable PDFs or editable documents.
  • Amendments supplied together with the relevant agreement.
  • Image-only scans, handwriting, and documents needing OCR cleanup are excluded.

Included work

  • Extract these 15 fields per agreement: parties; effective date; products or services covered; initial term and expiration; renewal mechanism and renewal term; nonrenewal notice requirement; termination rights and notice; stated pricing; price adjustment provisions; minimum commitments; payment terms; material service levels; service credits or stated penalties; insurance requirements; assignment restrictions.
  • Pricing extraction limited to 20 distinct price lines per agreement.
  • Page and section references for each extracted field.
  • A separate list of renewal and notice dates determinable from the documents.
  • "Not found", "ambiguous", or "conflicting" labels where appropriate.

Deliverables

  • Excel workbook with one summary row per agreement plus a detail tab.
  • Page and section references for every field.
  • Separate list of renewal and notice dates determinable from the documents.

Excluded

  • Legal advice or enforceability opinions.
  • Redlining or negotiation recommendations.
  • Market-standard comparisons.
  • Exhaustive price-book transcription.
  • Resolving conflicts through legal interpretation.
  • Ongoing renewal tracking.

Revision allowance

Reformatting or clarifying existing extracted fields. New agreements, amendments, or fields are additional scope.

Delivery clock rules

The delivery clock starts on the next business day after payment is received and all required inputs are in hand. Business days are Monday through Friday, excluding US federal holidays. If required inputs are missing or scope questions go unanswered, the clock pauses and you will be notified before work continues.

Client responsibilities

  • Supply complete, searchable agreements with amendments grouped per agreement.
  • Flag which renewal dates matter most, if any.
  • Confirm receipt and report issues within the revision window.

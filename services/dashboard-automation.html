Dashboard + Automation Package

Replace the recurring report you assemble by hand with a dashboard that refreshes on a schedule and emails you the result.

Platform note: this package is built on Google Sheets and Google Apps Script in your Google account. Power BI, Excel desktop automation, CRM integrations, and arbitrary API integrations are not included at this price. No ongoing monitoring or maintenance is included.

$1,490 / project
Delivery: ten business days

Key limits

  • Built on Google Sheets and Google Apps Script in the client's Google account only.
  • One recurring reporting process for one team: up to three source tabs and 25,000 total rows.
  • At most one shared join key; joins are one-to-one or many-to-one only.
  • No ongoing monitoring or maintenance is included.

Eligible inputs and limits

  • One recurring reporting process for one team.
  • Up to three source tabs across up to three Google Sheets files.
  • Up to 25,000 total populated source rows, with up to 30 columns per tab.
  • At most one shared join key.
  • Joins are one-to-one or many-to-one only.
  • The client maintains source data in the agreed layout.

Included work

  • Standardize dates, numeric formats, and headers, and remove exact duplicates.
  • Build one dashboard tab with up to eight KPIs.
  • Up to eight charts or tables and three filters.
  • Build one scheduled workflow that reads the agreed sources, refreshes the reporting tables, and emails one dashboard PDF.
  • One schedule: daily, weekly, or monthly.
  • Fixed distribution list of up to five email addresses.
  • One failure-notification email to the client's designated owner.
  • Install in the client-owned environment with two successful end-to-end test runs.

Deliverables

  • A working source-to-dashboard workflow installed in your Google account.
  • The editable dashboard and Apps Script code.
  • Written instructions covering source updates, schedule changes, ownership, and common errors.
  • A test record of the refresh and email delivery.

Excluded

  • Pulling data from email attachments, accounting tools, CRMs, websites, or external APIs.
  • Multiple workflows, approval chains, two-way sync, or writebacks.
  • Custom web apps or historical backfills.
  • Automatic adaptation if source layouts change.
  • Client software subscriptions and usage fees.

After handoff

No revision rounds are included. Only documented errors against the accepted scope qualify for correction. Report errors within five business days of delivery with examples or reproduction steps. Verified errors are corrected or refunded under the Error remedy. A corrected delivery has a three-business-day verification period for unresolved or newly introduced errors only.

Examples include an incorrect calculation or failure of the agreed workflow in the original client environment. Changed permissions, source layouts, vendor behavior, schedules, recipients, or reporting preferences after delivery are additional scope unless correcting our failure to implement the accepted specification.

No ongoing support or maintenance is included. Changes beyond error correction require a new written agreement and payment.

Delivery clock rules

The delivery clock starts on the next business day after payment is received and all required inputs are in hand. Business days are Monday through Friday, excluding US federal holidays. If required inputs are missing or scope questions go unanswered, the clock pauses and you will be notified before work continues.

Client responsibilities

  • Keep source data in the agreed layout.
  • Provide Google account access for installation.
  • Designate the owner who receives failure notifications.

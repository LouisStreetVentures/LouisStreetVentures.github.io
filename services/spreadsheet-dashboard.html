Spreadsheet Dashboard

A custom workbook for the numbers you need to track: cleaned source data, up to six KPIs, and charts with filters, plus written refresh instructions.

$590 / project
Delivery: five business days

Key limits

  • One file (Excel or Google Sheets) with up to three source tabs, 10,000 rows, and 25 columns.
  • One business function; no joins between different datasets.
  • Calculations limited to sums, counts, averages, ratios, and period comparisons.
  • Delivered as either Excel or Google Sheets (one format), chosen before payment.

Eligible inputs and limits

  • One Excel workbook or Google Sheets file.
  • Up to three source tabs with matching columns, combined by appending rows.
  • Up to 10,000 total rows and 25 columns.
  • One business function.
  • No joins between different datasets.

Included work

  • Basic preparation: headers, date and number formats, exact duplicate removal.
  • One dashboard tab.
  • Up to six KPIs, six charts or tables, and three filters.
  • Calculations limited to sums, counts, averages, ratios, and period comparisons.
  • One agreed refresh procedure.

Deliverables

  • One editable Excel workbook or Google Sheets file (format chosen before payment).
  • Written refresh instructions.
  • KPI validation against sample data.

Excluded

  • Scheduled refreshes or automated email.
  • External connections, macros, or custom scripts.
  • Financial forecasting or multi-user applications.
  • Delivering both Excel and Sheets versions.

Revision allowance

Up to three presentation adjustments and one change to an existing KPI calculation. New data sources, metrics, or deliverables are additional scope.

Submit one consolidated request within five business days of delivery. After a revised delivery, a three-business-day verification period covers unresolved or newly introduced material failures only. No ongoing support, maintenance, or separate error-correction period is included after completion. See the review and completion terms.

Delivery clock rules

The delivery clock starts on the next business day after payment is received and all required inputs are in hand. Business days are Monday through Friday, excluding US federal holidays. If required inputs are missing or scope questions go unanswered, the clock pauses and you will be notified before work continues.

Client responsibilities

  • Choose Excel or Google Sheets before payment.
  • Provide the source file with matching columns across tabs.
  • Confirm the KPIs and the refresh procedure.

Lead List Building

100 verified B2B contacts at 100 distinct companies that match your target customer profile, delivered as one CSV or Excel file.

$390 / project
Delivery: three business days

Key limits

  • 100 contacts at 100 distinct companies; one named contact per company.
  • Business emails verified with a verification service; catch-all, unknown, or invalid results do not count toward the 100.
  • No generic inboxes; guessed emails are never labeled as verified.
  • Feasibility of the targeting criteria is confirmed before payment.
Start intake ↗

Eligible inputs and limits

  • 100 contacts at 100 distinct companies.
  • One target customer profile.
  • One US geographic area (may be nationwide).
  • Up to three industry categories.
  • One company-size band.
  • Up to two accepted job titles or functions.
  • One exclusion list of up to 1,000 company domains.
  • Required fields per contact: company name, website, city/state, industry, contact name, contact title, business email, company/contact source URL, short explanation of fit, email verification status and date.
  • Quality standard: one named contact per company; each company meets the agreed observable criteria; no generic inboxes or guessed emails labeled as verified; business emails verified with a verification service; catch-all, unknown, or invalid results do not count toward the 100; size estimates labeled as estimates; one primary source link per lead; feasibility confirmed before payment.

Included work

  • Research 100 companies matching the agreed target customer profile.
  • Identify one named contact per company.
  • Verify business emails with a verification service.
  • Record the required fields and one primary source link per lead.
  • Confirm feasibility of the criteria before payment.

Deliverables

  • One CSV or Excel file with 100 contacts meeting the quality standard.

Excluded

  • Outreach or appointment setting.
  • Phone verification or direct-dial numbers.
  • Buying-intent research or personalized opening lines.
  • Guaranteed deliverability, responses, meetings, or sales.

After handoff

No revision rounds are included. Only documented errors against the accepted scope qualify for correction. Report errors within five business days of delivery with examples or reproduction steps. Verified errors are corrected or refunded under the Error remedy. A corrected delivery has a three-business-day verification period for unresolved or newly introduced errors only.

Examples include duplicates, contacts outside the agreed targeting criteria, or verification results that did not meet the agreed standard at delivery. A documented hard bounce during the reporting period may support an Error report; non-replies, lack of interest, later job changes, or a different target profile do not qualify. The general Error remedy applies without a separate replacement round or ten-contact cap.

No ongoing support or maintenance is included. Changes beyond error correction require a new written agreement and payment.

Delivery clock rules

The delivery clock starts on the next business day after payment is received and all required inputs are in hand. Business days are Monday through Friday, excluding US federal holidays. If required inputs are missing or scope questions go unanswered, the clock pauses and you will be notified before work continues.

Client responsibilities

  • Agree the target customer profile and observable criteria before payment.
  • Provide the exclusion list of company domains.
  • Report contacts that fail the agreed delivery specification within five business days, with documentation.

Spreadsheet Cleanup

Make an inconsistent spreadsheet easier to sort, filter, and use: standardized values, duplicates removed, and every change documented.

$190 / project
Delivery: two business days

Key limits

  • One file or tab: up to 500 data rows and 20 columns, one flat table.
  • No merged-cell layouts or multi-table worksheets.
  • Up to 25 explicit find-and-replace mappings.
  • Deduplication on one client-approved key with one agreed keep rule.
Start intake ↗

Eligible inputs and limits

  • One CSV or XLSX file, or one Google Sheets tab.
  • Up to 500 data rows excluding the header.
  • Up to 20 columns.
  • One flat table.
  • No merged-cell layouts or multi-table worksheets.

Included work

  • Trim whitespace and standardize agreed capitalization.
  • Standardize dates and numeric formats where unambiguous.
  • Remove exact duplicate rows.
  • Deduplicate on one client-approved key with one agreed keep rule.
  • Apply up to 25 explicit find-and-replace mappings.
  • Flag blanks, invalid formats, and ambiguous values.
  • Preserve an original-data copy.
  • Reconcile retained rows to the input.

Deliverables

  • One cleaned file in the agreed format.
  • One exceptions tab or file listing flagged issues.
  • Change summary showing input rows, output rows, duplicate removals, and flagged issues.

Excluded

  • Fuzzy entity matching or external research.
  • Data enrichment or OCR.
  • Merging files or repairing formulas and macros.
  • Building dashboards.
  • Verifying the truth of supplied facts.
  • Filling missing values by inference.

After handoff

No revision rounds are included. Only documented errors against the accepted scope qualify for correction. Report errors within five business days of delivery with examples or reproduction steps. Verified errors are corrected or refunded under the Error remedy. A corrected delivery has a three-business-day verification period for unresolved or newly introduced errors only.

Examples include failure to remove an exact duplicate under the agreed rule, an incorrectly transformed value, or accidental loss of a retained row. New cleaning rules, replacement source files, and preference changes are additional work.

No ongoing support or maintenance is included. Changes beyond error correction require a new written agreement and payment.

Delivery clock rules

The delivery clock starts on the next business day after payment is received and all required inputs are in hand. Business days are Monday through Friday, excluding US federal holidays. If required inputs are missing or scope questions go unanswered, the clock pauses and you will be notified before work continues.

Client responsibilities

  • Approve the deduplication key and keep rule before work begins.
  • Provide the find-and-replace mappings (up to 25).
  • Confirm the output format.

Fixed-price service

Supplier Spend Analysis

See where supplier spending is concentrated, which purchases are fragmented, and where consolidation could help. A fixed-scope analysis of your purchasing or invoice data with clear, prioritized findings.

$2,490 / project
Delivery: five business days

Key limits

  • One legal entity and one reporting currency, already present in the data. No currency conversion.
  • Up to 12 consecutive months and 25,000 transaction rows.
  • Invoice data or payment data: reconciling the two is excluded.
  • Amounts must already use consistent tax and credit treatment.
Start intake ↗

Eligible inputs and limits

  • Data for one legal entity.
  • One reporting currency, already present in the data. Currency conversion is not included.
  • Up to 12 consecutive months of transactions.
  • Up to 25,000 transaction rows.
  • Up to 250 distinct supplier names before normalization.
  • Up to three CSV or XLSX files with matching column structures.
  • One optional supplier master file with up to 250 records.
  • Required fields: supplier name, transaction or invoice date, and amount. Invoice number is also required for invoice-level checks.
  • Either invoice data or payment data. Reconciling the two is excluded.
  • Amounts must already use consistent tax and credit treatment.

Included work

  • Normalize supplier names, dates, and category labels.
  • Build up to 15 mutually exclusive top-level categories with an agreed mapping, with one primary category per supplier.
  • Reconcile output totals to the submitted files and document any exclusions.
  • Provide views by supplier, category, and month.
  • Segment spend into three tiers using an agreed rule (default: cumulative 80/15/5 bands).
  • Analyze concentration, fragmented spend, and monthly changes.
  • Run duplicate-invoice checks where the fields exist.
  • Individually review the top 20 suppliers.

Deliverables

  • Excel workbook with cleaned transactions, the category mapping, summary views, and segmentation.
  • Written report of up to six pages.
  • Up to five prioritized findings, each with supporting calculations, stated assumptions, and a proposed next action.

Excluded

  • SKU-level price benchmarking.
  • External quotes, negotiations, or implementation of changes.
  • Contract review or legal analysis.
  • Tax analysis or accounting reconciliation.
  • Currency conversion.
  • Guaranteed savings.
  • Extracting transactions from PDFs or invoice images.

Revision allowance

One revision round: a single correction to the category or segmentation rules, followed by regeneration of the existing outputs. New data, categories, or deliverables are additional scope.

Delivery clock rules

The delivery clock starts on the next business day after payment is received and all required inputs are in hand. Business days are Monday through Friday, excluding US federal holidays. If required inputs are missing or scope questions go unanswered, the clock pauses and you will be notified before work continues.

Client responsibilities

  • Provide the data files in the agreed format with the required fields present.
  • Confirm the category mapping and tier rule before analysis begins.
  • Answer scope questions promptly so the delivery clock keeps running.
